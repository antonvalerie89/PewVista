1. GETTING STARTED
- Start PewVista from its desktop or Start Menu shortcut.
- The main window is the control screen used by the operator. The live
  (secondary) screen is what the congregation sees on the second monitor
  or projector.
- Before a service, connect the projector and set Windows to Extend the
  displays (Windows key + P > Extend), not Duplicate.
- New installations include a 15-day free trial with all features. The
  window title shows the days left.

2. THE MAIN WINDOW
- Top row, three panels:
  - Info: the service date, time, and preacher, with Save and Print.
  - Secondary Screen: a live preview of what the congregation sees, and
    the switch that turns the live screen on or off.
  - Actions: saved services and quick actions for the current service.
- Bottom area: the content tabs - Display, Welcome, Library, Preparation,
  Bible, Add Bible, and Translation.
- Menu bar: Settings, History, Licenses, Data, Members, and Help.

3. PLANNING A SERVICE (INFO PANEL)
- Choose the service date with the calendar (Month, Year, Today, Apply).
- Choose the time slot (for example Morning or Evening) and enter the
  preacher's name.
- Add items from the content tabs with Send to Display. They appear in
  the Display queue in order.
- Click Save to store the service so it can be recalled later. Click
  Print to print the order of service (see section 13).

4. THE DISPLAY QUEUE (DISPLAY TAB)
- The Display Queue lists every item planned for the service: welcome
  notes, songs, preparation blocks, and Bible passages.
- Select an item to see it in the preview; it is shown live when the
  secondary screen is on.
- Down and Up change the order; Remove takes an item out of the queue.
- Previous and Next step through the slides (verses, blocks, or pages) of
  the selected item. Use these during the service.
- Refresh reloads the queue if another tab changed it.

5. WELCOME TAB
- Welcome Notes: write, save, and reuse welcome messages. Use New, Save,
  and Delete to manage them.
- Upcoming Birthdays: lists members with birthdays coming up, taken from
  the Members register.
- Birthday Message: the text shown with the birthday names.
- Combine Birthdays With Welcome Text: shows the welcome note and the
  birthdays together on one screen.
- Activate Scroll: scrolls long text slowly upwards on the live screen.
- Background: choose the image or video behind the welcome text.
- Preview shows the result; Send to Display adds it to the queue.

6. LIBRARY TAB (SONGS)
- Library Songs: the song list. Type in the search box to filter by
  title. A starter set of public-domain hymns is included.
- Song Editor fields:
  - Title, Key, Author, and Year.
  - Music License: Public Domain, CCLI, OneLicense, Individual, or
    Copyrighted. For Individual, a License info box appears for the
    license details. Public Domain songs show no license line on screen.
  - Background: the image or video used for this song.
  - Verses: each part of the song is one slide. Use the dropdown next to
    each part to mark it as Verse, Chorus, Pre-Chorus, Bridge, Refrain,
    Intro, Outro, Tag, or Ending. The Display queue then shows names such
    as "Verse 1", "Chorus", and "Bridge". Use Add Verse to add parts and
    Remove to delete one.
- New Song starts an empty song, Save stores it, Clear empties the
  editor, and Delete removes the song from the library.
- Show Live puts the selected song straight on the live screen; Send to
  Display adds it to the queue.
- For licensed songs, the live screen shows the song key, title, author,
  and your church's license number in the corner, as required by
  licensing bodies. Record the numbers under Licenses (section 15).

7. PREPARATION TAB
- Use this for sermon points, announcements, or any free text.
- Theme Header: the heading shown at the top of the screen.
- Text Block: the text for one slide. Display Blocks lists the slides.
- Background: the image or video behind the text.
- Preview / Details shows the result; Send to Display adds it to the
  queue.

8. BIBLE TAB
- Bible Comparison: show one passage in up to three translations side by
  side.
- Select Bibles: choose which translations to compare.
- Book, Chapter, Start, and End: choose the passage.
- Background: the image or video behind the verses.
- Send to Display adds the passage to the queue. Use Previous and Next in
  the Display tab to move through the verses.
- Only Bibles that may be shown in public are included. See Help > Bible
  Credits and Licenses.

9. ADD BIBLE TAB
- Import Bible Files: add a Bible from a file on this computer.
- Import From URL: download a Bible from a web address.
- Refresh updates the list; Open Folder shows the Bible files.
- Selected Bible shows the type, size, source, and license record.
- License tools: Create License Note, Open License Note, Open Licenses,
  and Source Verified record that you have permission to display the
  Bible. Only import Bibles your church is allowed to use.
- Online Discoveries lists Bibles found online with their publisher and
  status.

10. TRANSLATION TAB
- Study the original Greek and Hebrew words of a verse.
- Choose the Bible, Book, Chapter, and Verse.
- The table shows each Original Word with its English Meaning and
  Afrikaans Meaning.

11. THE LIVE (SECONDARY) SCREEN
- Turn the live screen on with the switch in the Secondary Screen panel.
- PewVista shows the live screen through its own copy of OBS Studio.
  Setup prepares OBS; on PCs without OBS an internet connection is needed
  during Setup or the first time the live screen starts.
- PewVista starts OBS quietly when it opens, so the first item appears
  quickly. OBS appears in the system tray, not as a window.
- Keep the projector connected before turning the live screen on.
- Settings > Secondary Screen sets the font, outline size, text color,
  and key size for the live screen.
- Without a license, the live screen shows a red NOT LICENSED marking
  (see section 19).

12. SAVED SERVICES AND HISTORY
- Saved Services (Actions panel) lists services saved for the selected
  date. Recall Selected loads a saved service into the Display queue.
- History > Open History shows earlier services with their details.
  Recall Selected loads one; Delete Selected Service... removes it.

13. PRINTING
- Print (Info panel) opens the Print Display Queue window.
- Choose the Printer and the Content: the Selected item or the Entire
  queue.
- Song Lists prints song titles only; Text Size sets the print size.
- Preview shows each page (use Previous and Next); set Copies, then
  click Print.

14. MEMBERS
- Members (menu bar) opens the member register: Members List, Member
  Details, and Members Actions.
- New Member clears the form; Add Member saves a new person; Update
  Member saves changes; Remove Member deletes the selected person.
- Birthdays entered here appear in the Welcome tab.
- Member details are encrypted on this computer. Protect access to the
  PC with a Windows password.

15. MUSIC LICENSES
- Licenses (menu bar) records your church's CCLI License Number and
  OneLicense Number.
- These numbers are shown on the live screen with licensed songs.
- Add Later closes the window without saving.

16. SETTINGS
- Settings (menu bar) has two tabs:
  - Main Screen: the theme and the operator window's font family, style,
    and size.
  - Secondary Screen: the live screen's font, outline size, text color,
    and key size. Choose None to hide the song key on the live screen.

17. YOUR DATA: BACKUPS, EXPORT, AND IMPORT
- All songs, services, members, and settings are stored on this computer
  in the shared data folder chosen during installation. Updates and
  reinstalls keep them.
- PewVista makes an automatic daily backup in that folder.
- Data > Open Background Folder: add your own images and videos for
  backgrounds. Still images, GIFs, and videos are supported.
- Data > Export Databases...: saves a ZIP with the databases you select
  (Select All or Select None). When members are included you choose a
  password, and the same password is needed to import them.
- Data > Import Databases...: restores a ZIP or moves data to another
  computer. Choose the Import mode for each database.
- Data > Export Service Day(s)... and Import Service Day(s)...: move
  selected saved services to another computer.
- Keep a recent export on a USB drive or another computer.

18. CHURCH NAME, BACKGROUND, AND ICON
- Help > Church Name, Background and Icon... sets the church name shown
  in the window title, the default background, and the app icon (.ico).
- Use Browse... to choose a file and Clear to return to the PewVista
  default. Changes apply immediately and survive updates.

19. LICENSE AND SUBSCRIPTION
- PewVista starts with a 15-day free trial on each PC.
- A subscription costs {PRICE} and covers 2 PCs.
- Open Help > License and Subscription... to see the license status.
- How to Get a License explains the steps. In short:
  - Pay PewVista (EFT; email pewvista52@gmail.com for banking details).
  - Click Copy ID and send the copied text (church name and PC ID) with
    your proof of payment to PewVista. Do this on each PC.
  - Paste the license code you receive into the box and click Activate.
- PayPal subscriptions: enter the subscription ID PayPal emails you
  (starts with I-) and click Activate, where available. These PCs must
  connect to the internet at least once every 30 days.
- Without a license, PewVista keeps working, but the live screen shows a
  red NOT LICENSED marking. Your data is never deleted.
- Remove from this PC frees the license so it can be used on another PC.

20. UPDATES
- PewVista checks for updates about once a day and asks before
  installing. Help > Check for Updates... checks immediately.
- Help > Install Update... installs an update ZIP you received from
  PewVista. Accept the Terms and Conditions when asked.
- The app and OBS close while the update installs, and PewVista restarts
  afterwards. Your data, settings, and licenses are kept, and changed
  files are backed up first.
- The installed version is shown at the bottom of the Help menu.

21. TROUBLESHOOTING
- Church name, background, or icon missing after an update: set them
  again under Help > Church Name, Background and Icon.
- Live screen does not appear: check the projector cable and that
  Windows is set to Extend, then turn the live screen off and on again,
  or restart PewVista.
- "OBS Studio is not on this PC yet": connect to the internet and start
  the live screen again; PewVista downloads OBS (about 185 MB).
- An update fails: the previous version is restored automatically and
  your data is untouched. Close PewVista, wait a minute, and try again.
- Red NOT LICENSED marking: see section 19.
- For help, email pewvista52@gmail.com with the version number from the
  Help menu.
