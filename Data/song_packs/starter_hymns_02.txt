# The Old Rugged Cross | George Bennard | 1913
[Verse]
On a hill far away stood an old rugged cross,
The emblem of suffering and shame;
And I love that old cross where the dearest and best
For a world of lost sinners was slain.
[Chorus]
So I'll cherish the old rugged cross,
Till my trophies at last I lay down;
I will cling to the old rugged cross,
And exchange it some day for a crown.
[Verse]
O that old rugged cross, so despised by the world,
Has a wondrous attraction for me;
For the dear Lamb of God left his glory above
To bear it to dark Calvary.
[Verse]
In that old rugged cross, stained with blood so divine,
A wondrous beauty I see,
For 'twas on that old cross Jesus suffered and died,
To pardon and sanctify me.
# What a Friend We Have in Jesus | Joseph Scriven | 1855
[Verse]
What a friend we have in Jesus,
All our sins and griefs to bear!
What a privilege to carry
Everything to God in prayer!
O what peace we often forfeit,
O what needless pain we bear,
All because we do not carry
Everything to God in prayer!
[Verse]
Have we trials and temptations?
Is there trouble anywhere?
We should never be discouraged;
Take it to the Lord in prayer.
Can we find a friend so faithful
Who will all our sorrows share?
Jesus knows our every weakness;
Take it to the Lord in prayer.
[Verse]
Are we weak and heavy laden,
Cumbered with a load of care?
Precious Saviour, still our refuge,
Take it to the Lord in prayer.
Do thy friends despise, forsake thee?
Take it to the Lord in prayer!
In His arms He'll take and shield thee;
Thou wilt find a solace there.
# Just As I Am | Charlotte Elliott | 1835
[Verse]
Just as I am, without one plea,
But that Thy blood was shed for me,
And that Thou bidd'st me come to Thee,
O Lamb of God, I come, I come.
[Verse]
Just as I am, and waiting not
To rid my soul of one dark blot,
To Thee whose blood can cleanse each spot,
O Lamb of God, I come, I come.
[Verse]
Just as I am, though tossed about
With many a conflict, many a doubt,
Fightings and fears within, without,
O Lamb of God, I come, I come.
[Verse]
Just as I am, Thou wilt receive,
Wilt welcome, pardon, cleanse, relieve;
Because Thy promise I believe,
O Lamb of God, I come, I come.
# Nearer, My God, to Thee | Sarah Flower Adams | 1841
[Verse]
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer to Thee!
E'en though it be a cross
That raiseth me;
Still all my song shall be,
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer to Thee!
[Verse]
Though like the wanderer,
The sun gone down,
Darkness be over me,
My rest a stone;
Yet in my dreams I'd be
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer to Thee!
[Verse]
There let the way appear,
Steps unto heaven;
All that Thou sendest me
In mercy given;
Angels to beckon me
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer to Thee!
[Verse]
Then with my waking thoughts
Bright with Thy praise,
Out of my stony griefs
Bethel I'll raise;
So by my woes to be
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer, my God, to Thee,
Nearer to Thee!
# Leaning on the Everlasting Arms | Elisha A. Hoffman | 1887
[Verse]
What a fellowship, what a joy divine,
Leaning on the everlasting arms;
What a blessedness, what a peace is mine,
Leaning on the everlasting arms.
[Chorus]
Leaning, leaning, safe and secure from all alarms;
Leaning, leaning, leaning on the everlasting arms.
[Verse]
Oh, how sweet to walk in this pilgrim way,
Leaning on the everlasting arms;
Oh, how bright the path grows from day to day,
Leaning on the everlasting arms.
[Verse]
What have I to dread, what have I to fear,
Leaning on the everlasting arms?
I have blessed peace with my Lord so near,
Leaning on the everlasting arms.
# Trust and Obey | John H. Sammis | 1887
[Verse]
When we walk with the Lord
In the light of His Word,
What a glory He sheds on our way!
While we do His good will,
He abides with us still,
And with all who will trust and obey.
[Chorus]
Trust and obey, for there's no other way
To be happy in Jesus, but to trust and obey.
[Verse]
Not a burden we bear,
Not a sorrow we share,
But our toil He doth richly repay;
Not a grief or a loss,
Not a frown or a cross,
But is blest if we trust and obey.
[Verse]
Then in fellowship sweet
We will sit at His feet,
Or we'll walk by His side in the way;
What He says we will do,
Where He sends we will go;
Never fear, only trust and obey.
# I Need Thee Every Hour | Annie S. Hawks | 1872
[Verse]
I need Thee every hour,
Most gracious Lord;
No tender voice like Thine
Can peace afford.
[Chorus]
I need Thee, O I need Thee;
Every hour I need Thee;
O bless me now, my Saviour,
I come to Thee.
[Verse]
I need Thee every hour,
Stay Thou near by;
Temptations lose their power
When Thou art nigh.
[Verse]
I need Thee every hour,
In joy or pain;
Come quickly and abide,
Or life is vain.
[Verse]
I need Thee every hour,
Most Holy One;
O make me Thine indeed,
Thou blessed Son.
# Redeemed, How I Love to Proclaim It | Fanny J. Crosby | 1882
[Verse]
Redeemed, how I love to proclaim it!
Redeemed by the blood of the Lamb;
Redeemed through His infinite mercy,
His child, and forever I am.
[Chorus]
Redeemed, redeemed,
Redeemed by the blood of the Lamb;
Redeemed, redeemed,
His child, and forever I am.
[Verse]
Redeemed and so happy in Jesus,
No language my rapture can tell;
I know that the light of His presence
Is with me wherever I dwell.
[Verse]
I think of my Saviour and Jesus,
Who died on the cross, and I shall see
I'll sing with the angels of heaven
Forever and forever with Thee.
# Jesus Paid It All | Elvina M. Hall | 1865
[Verse]
I hear the Saviour say,
"Thy strength indeed is small;
Child of weakness, watch and pray,
Find in Me thine all in all."
[Chorus]
Jesus paid it all,
All to Him I owe;
Sin had left a crimson stain,
He washed it white as snow.
[Verse]
Lord, now indeed I find
Thy power, and Thine alone,
Can change the leper's spots
And melt the heart of stone.
[Verse]
For nothing good have I
Whereby Thy grace to claim;
I'll wash my garments white
In the blood of Calvary's Lamb.
[Verse]
And when, before the throne,
I stand in Him complete,
"Jesus died my soul to save,"
My lips shall still repeat.
