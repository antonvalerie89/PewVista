PewVista Privacy and Security Statement

This statement explains what information PewVista stores, where it is stored, how it is protected, and what your organisation is responsible for.

1. What PewVista Stores

PewVista may store information entered by your organisation, including:

Member names
Surnames
Telephone numbers
Addresses
Email addresses
Dates of birth
Song libraries
Saved services
Service history
Welcome notes
CCLI information
One Licence information
Application settings
Background images
Videos
Other content added by authorised users

Only information entered by your organisation is stored.

2. Where Data Is Stored

PewVista stores data locally on computers owned or managed by your organisation.

Data is normally stored within the shared data folder selected during installation.

Backup copies may also be stored within the backup locations configured by your organisation.

PewVista does not require an online account, cloud platform, or centralised database operated by the developer.

The developer does not automatically collect, receive, access, review, or store your organisation's data.

3. Internet Usage

PewVista operates primarily offline.

Internet access is only used when an authorised user chooses features that require downloading content from an external source, such as importing supported files from a web address.

Unless specifically requested by the user, PewVista does not transmit church member information, song libraries, service data, or personal information to the developer.

4. Who Can Access Data

Access to information stored by PewVista depends on the permissions configured on the computer where the software is installed.

The organisation is responsible for:

Managing user accounts
Granting access rights
Removing access when required
Protecting administrator accounts

Sensitive member information may be encrypted by PewVista. However, the organisation remains responsible for overall computer security and physical access to the device.

5. Data Security Measures

PewVista includes security features designed to help protect stored information.

These may include:

Local encryption of sensitive member information
Password-protected exports
Access restrictions based on Windows permissions
Secure update verification
Digitally signed updates
Backup management features
Protected communication between application components

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Organisations should implement appropriate security practices, including:

Strong passwords
Device security
Antivirus protection
Drive encryption
Regular backups
User access controls
6. Software Updates

Updates are not installed automatically unless specifically enabled by the organisation.

Where supported, update packages are digitally verified before installation.

Backup copies may be created before updates are applied.

The organisation is responsible for deciding whether and when updates are installed.

7. Your Organisation's Responsibilities

Your organisation is responsible for:

Ensuring lawful collection of personal information
Obtaining required consent where applicable
Maintaining accurate records
Protecting personal information
Responding to requests for correction or deletion
Managing data retention periods
Following applicable privacy and data-protection laws

This responsibility remains with your organisation regardless of the features used within PewVista.

8. Privacy and Data Protection Compliance

Privacy laws differ between countries and regions.

Examples include:

POPIA (South Africa)
GDPR (European Union)
UK GDPR (United Kingdom)
Privacy Act requirements in various jurisdictions

The organisation using PewVista is solely responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable privacy, data-protection, and information-governance laws.

The developer does not provide legal advice regarding compliance obligations.

9. Developer Access to Your Data

The developer does not routinely access, collect, monitor, analyse, sell, share, or process customer data stored within PewVista.

If technical support is requested, the organisation may voluntarily provide exports, screenshots, log files, or backups for troubleshooting purposes.

Such information is supplied at the organisation's discretion.

10. Limitation of Responsibility

PewVista is a software tool.

The organisation using PewVista remains the controller, owner, and manager of all data entered into the software.

The developer is not responsible for:

How information is collected
How information is used
Decisions made using stored information
User account management
Loss of passwords
Data breaches caused by third parties
Device theft
Improper configuration
Failure to maintain backups
Failure to comply with applicable privacy laws
