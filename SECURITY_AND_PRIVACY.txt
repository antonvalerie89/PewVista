PewVista Privacy and Security Statement

This statement explains what information PewVista stores, where it is stored, how it is protected, and what your organization is responsible for.

1. What PewVista Stores

PewVista may store information entered by your organization, including:

- Member names
- Surnames
- Telephone numbers
- Addresses
- Email addresses
- Dates of birth
- Song libraries
- Saved services
- Service history
- Welcome notes
- CCLI information
- One License information
- Application settings
- License and trial information
- Background images
- Videos
- Other content added by authorized users

Only information entered by your organization is stored.

2. Where Data Is Stored

PewVista stores data locally on computers owned or managed by your organization.

Data is normally stored within the shared data folder selected during installation.

Backup copies may also be stored within the backup locations configured by your organization.

PewVista does not require an online account, cloud platform, or centralized database operated by the developer for your organization's data.

The developer does not automatically collect, receive, access, review, or store your organization's data, such as member details, song libraries, or services.

Licensing records

To manage subscriptions, PewVista and the developer keep the limited licensing information described in section 3: the organization's name and contact details, subscription or payment references, license periods, and the PC IDs of licensed computers. A PC ID is an anonymous code derived from the computer; it contains no names or files. License and trial information is also stored on each licensed computer.

3. Internet Usage

PewVista operates primarily offline. It uses the internet only for the following:

- Licensing: when a PC is activated with a PayPal subscription, and about once a day afterwards while online, PewVista sends the subscription ID, the PC ID, the computer's name, and the PewVista version to the PewVista license service to confirm the subscription. License codes issued for direct payment need no internet connection.
- Update checks: about once a day, PewVista checks whether a newer version is available. Only the request itself is sent; no personal information is included.
- OBS Studio: during installation, or the first time the secondary screen starts, PewVista may download OBS Studio from its official website if it is not already on the computer.
- Features chosen by an authorized user that download content from an external source, such as importing supported files from a web address.

PewVista never transmits church member information, song libraries, service data, or other content to the developer unless an authorized user specifically chooses to send it, for example for technical support.

4. Who Can Access Data

Access to information stored by PewVista depends on the permissions configured on the computer where the software is installed.

The organization is responsible for:

- Managing user accounts
- Granting access rights
- Removing access when required
- Protecting administrator accounts

Sensitive member information may be encrypted by PewVista. However, the organization remains responsible for overall computer security and physical access to the device.

5. Data Security Measures

PewVista includes security features designed to help protect stored information.

These may include:

- Local encryption of sensitive member information
- Password-protected exports
- Access restrictions based on Windows permissions
- Secure update verification
- Digitally signed updates
- Backup management features
- Protected communication between application components

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Organizations should implement appropriate security practices, including:

- Strong passwords
- Device security
- Antivirus protection
- Drive encryption
- Regular backups
- User access controls

6. Software Updates

PewVista checks for new versions automatically but never installs an update without the confirmation of an authorized user.

Update packages are digitally verified before installation; altered or unsigned packages are refused.

Backup copies are created before updates are applied.

The organization is responsible for deciding whether and when updates are installed.

7. Your Organization's Responsibilities

Your organization is responsible for:

- Ensuring lawful collection of personal information
- Obtaining required consent where applicable
- Maintaining accurate records
- Protecting personal information
- Responding to requests for correction or deletion
- Managing data retention periods
- Following applicable privacy and data-protection laws

This responsibility remains with your organization regardless of the features used within PewVista.

8. Privacy and Data Protection Compliance

Privacy laws differ between countries and regions.

Examples include:

- POPIA (South Africa)
- GDPR (European Union)
- UK GDPR (United Kingdom)
- Privacy Act requirements in various jurisdictions

The organization using PewVista is solely responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable privacy, data-protection, and information-governance laws.

The developer does not provide legal advice regarding compliance obligations.

9. Developer Access to Your Data

The developer does not routinely access, collect, monitor, analyze, sell, share, or process customer data stored within PewVista.

If technical support is requested, the organization may voluntarily provide exports, screenshots, log files, or backups for troubleshooting purposes.

Such information is supplied at the organization's discretion.

10. Limitation of Responsibility

PewVista is a software tool.

The organization using PewVista remains the controller, owner, and manager of all data entered into the software.

The developer is not responsible for:

- How information is collected
- How information is used
- Decisions made using stored information
- User account management
- Loss of passwords
- Data breaches caused by third parties
- Device theft
- Improper configuration
- Failure to maintain backups
- Failure to comply with applicable privacy laws
