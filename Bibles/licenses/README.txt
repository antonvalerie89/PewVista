Bible source and license notes

Purpose:
Keep a local record of where each Bible file came from and what licensing or usage position has been verified for that specific Bible.

Layout:
- `ebible.org.txt` is the provider summary note.
- Each downloaded Bible should also have its own `*.license.txt` record in this folder.
- Discovery notes for versions that were found online but not yet downloaded may also be kept here.

Notes:
- These notes are for internal traceability and later conversion work.
- If a Bible's exact license has not been individually verified yet, mark that clearly instead of guessing.
- Before redistributing converted Bible content outside this local workspace, re-check the exact terms for that Bible translation.
